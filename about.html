Where Healing Becomes Art

A youth-driven initiative creating a safe, understanding, and compassionate space for mental health support.

Our Story

Kintsugi Youth is a youth-driven initiative founded by @its_annikaagg. At Kintsugi, healing becomes art—as it is a Japanese art form of turning broken pieces into beautiful art structures using gold lining, just how we aim to help people use their own emotions to become a part of themselves, accept themselves as who they are.

Kintsugi Youth is a youth-centered psychological foundation dedicated to creating a safe, understanding, and compassionate space for mental health support. We help young people see that their emotions, struggles, and scars are not flaws to be hidden, but rather the very places where their unique strength, beauty, and wisdom emerge.

The Kintsugi Philosophy

The kintsugi philosophy teaches us that damage and history are integral parts of an object's beauty. When we apply this perspective to young people, we recognize that their struggles, traumas, and setbacks are not marks of shame—they are the very places where strength, resilience, and wisdom are forged.

At the heart of kintsugi is the belief that brokenness is not something to hide or be ashamed of. It is something to celebrate, repair, and honor. Our programs reflect this philosophy by helping young people identify their "cracks" and transform them into sources of power and beauty.

"The wound is the place where the light enters you."

— Rumi

The kintsugi process—where broken pieces are honored, repaired with gold, and displayed with pride—inspires our approach to healing.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

We help young people to express themselves freely and find healing through creative outlets, because emotions are not cringe—they are a part of everyday life and everyone should be reminded of happiness, sadness, struggles, wins and losses. Our programs are free for all youth, student-run, and student-driven—because the best support comes from peers who truly understand.

🔮

Our Vision

A world where every young person sees their emotions and imperfections not as flaws but as golden seams that contribute to their unique beauty, strength, and capacity for healing and helping others.

💎

Our Values

  • Youth-Led: A student-run initiative where young people lead the way in shaping our programs and sharing their voices in the broader conversation about mental health.
  • Free Access: All our programs are completely free for participants. We believe healing should never have a price tag.
  • Peer-Centered: The best support comes from peers who truly understand. We foster safe, understanding, and compassionate communities.
  • Creative Expression: Art opens doors that words cannot, providing pathways to healing, self-discovery, and empowerment.
