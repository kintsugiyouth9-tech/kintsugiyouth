The Philosophy of Kintsugi

Inspired by the ancient Japanese art, we believe that our broken places are where our greatest strength and beauty emerge.

What is Kintsugi?

Kintsugi is the centuries-old Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with lacquer mixed with powdered gold. Rather than hiding the damage, the philosophy embraces the flaws as part of the object's history, creating a unique and beautiful piece.

Kintsugi Youth applies this profound philosophy to the lives of young people. We help them see that their struggles, setbacks, and scars are not flaws to be ashamed of, but rather the very places where their unique strength, beauty, and resilience shine through.

"When we embrace our cracks, we allow the light—and the gold—to enter."
